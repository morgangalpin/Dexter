# 007.2 — 3D Printed Parts

This document is the **de-duplicated print list for one complete robot**: every 3D-printed part, once, with
the quantity to print and a pointer to its model file. It is the fabrication counterpart to
[007.1-Parts-Catalog.md](007.1-Parts-Catalog.md), which covers everything that is bought rather than
printed.

It is a **derived artifact**, generated from [007-Bill-of-Materials.md](007-Bill-of-Materials.md). See
[Regenerating this list](#regenerating-this-list).

| | Distinct parts | Pieces to print |

Tooling pieces are extra copies of robot parts plus three jigs; they are counted separately so a build

## Material

**Onyx (Markforged)** is the material of record for this generation of the design — the previous
generation printed in PLA, HD and later in Onyx. Parts whose model filename carries **"CF"** are printed
with **continuous carbon fibre reinforcement** (Markforged Mark Two class), not plain Onyx.

The printed parts are **scaffolding, not primary structure** — carbon fibre strakes and tubes
([007.1 §5](007.1-Parts-Catalog.md#5-structural-composites-and-metal-stock)) carry load between joints, and
the printed bodies locate them ([004](004-Mechanical-Architecture.md)). Two consequences bound any material
substitution:

- **Printed threads and thin captures strip easily.** [004](004-Mechanical-Architecture.md) limits torque
  throughout for this reason. A weaker filament narrows an already narrow margin.
- **The [base mounting plate](007.1-Parts-Catalog.md#c-509--base-mounting-plate-stock) must not be
  printed** in any material — it is the one load-bearing metal part in the robot.

⚠️ **Layer height, wall count, infill, and orientation are not in the design record.** `[TBD]` — the
original parts were produced on Markforged equipment with settings that were never published. Builders
using FDM machines must derive them. Bearing bores and CF strake slots are press/bond fits, so dimensional
accuracy on those features governs. Tracked as [DC-11](009-Design-Completion.md#procurement-data).

### Part identifiers

Three numbering schemes appear in this document, and only one names every part:

- **`PBS #`** — **the authoritative part number.** It is the only scheme covering all 70 printed parts, and
  the one [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md), [007.1](007.1-Parts-Catalog.md), [008](008-Assembly.md) and the
  [`Hardware/Models/`](../Hardware/Models/README.md) file names all key on. A part number alone locates its
  file.
- **`HDI-` / `TI1-` CAD IDs** — body names in the GLTF model, given in the `CAD ID` column where known.
  They cover only 13 of 70 parts, so they identify a part but cannot enumerate the set.
- **`KP` / `KA` numbers** — the superseded **v1** design in
  [`Hardware/Models/Reference/onshape-v1/`](../Hardware/Models/Reference/onshape-v1/README.md). These belong
  to no current part and are reference geometry only.

### Model-vs-BOM discrepancies

Two mismatches between the CAD model and [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md) are unresolved. Neither blocks a
build, but both should be checked against the model set before printing:

- **`HDI-311-006C_J2StatorHolderCap_ConeDrive`** appears in the CAD model as a distinct printed part but has
  **no row in [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md)**. It is plausibly a companion cap to the Pivot Stator Holder
  (`#200-002`). If the model set ships it separately, it is a missing BOM line.
- **`HDI-610-006_MotorShaftCoupler` is instanced 4 times in the CAD model**, where
  [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md) calls for **3** Wave Gen Couplers (one per strain-wave drive). Either the
  fourth instance is a scene duplicate or the BOM is one short.

Both are recorded under [DC-11](009-Design-Completion.md#procurement-data).

## Regenerating this list

This document is derived. **Do not hand-edit quantities** — regenerate them.

### When to regenerate

Whenever [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md) changes, or when the model archives are revised (a new Thingiverse
update, an OnShape release). A model revision that changes a part's geometry without changing its quantity
still requires the [Source](#model-file-sources) table to be re-checked.

### Procedure

1. **Extract every row typed `3D print`** from the subassembly tables in
   [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md). Parse the tables rather than reading them by eye.

2. **Apply per-subassembly multipliers.**
   [007.3](007-Bill-of-Materials.md#0073-harmonic-drive-motors) is quoted *per motor and is built twice*.
   Its Wave Gen Coupler / Flex Spline Attach / Flex Spline Cap rows therefore contribute 2 each, which
   combine with [007.8](007-Bill-of-Materials.md#0078-external-gear)'s 1 to give 3 — one per strain-wave
   drive. Sanity-check group totals against the physical count of drives, joints, or connectors they serve.

3. **Resolve every non-numeric quantity.** [007](007-Bill-of-Materials.md) contains "as needed" and
   "per joint". A print list cannot carry those — resolve each to a number, state the rule that produced it
   (*one strain-relief pair per 6-pin connector*), and flag it if the rule is a judgement call.

4. **Separate tooling from robot structure.** Glue-rig and solder-jig parts are extra copies or jigs.
   Carry them as a separate total so a build that skips the jigs does not over-print. Never fold them into
   the robot count.

5. **Reconcile against the CAD model.** Compare the extracted list to the `HDI-`/`TI1-` part names in
   `dde/HDIMeterModel.gltf` and the OnShape assembly. Record every part present in one and absent from the
   other under [Model-vs-BOM discrepancies](#model-vs-bom-discrepancies) — do not silently adopt either
   side. The GLTF is a kinematic-and-skin model, so absence from it is not by itself evidence a part does
   not exist.

6. **Re-check the [source archives](#model-file-sources).** Confirm each link resolves and note which
   archive currently holds each family. Where a part appears in both the HD set and the HD update, the
   **update supersedes**.

7. **Carry the `CAD ID` column forward.** It is the durable identifier; PBS numbers are legacy and contain
   known collisions (see [007.1 §6](007.1-Parts-Catalog.md#6-fasteners)). Fill in a `CAD ID` whenever one
   is recovered, and never delete one.

8. **Update the [totals](#totals)** and record the change in [CHANGES.md](../CHANGES.md).
